What people in Hunt Valley call about.

Most of the calls we field from this area start from one of a few recognizable signs. Often it is droppings turning up somewhere they were not before. None of those alone tells us mouse or rat, which is why the inspection comes before any treatment.

  • Scratching or movement heard once the house is quiet.
  • Droppings discovered somewhere they clearly should not be.
  • A sudden musty or ammonia-like smell with no obvious source.
  • Chewed packaging, insulation or wiring discovered during unrelated work.

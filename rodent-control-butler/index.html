What the local housing pattern means here.

In older houses, foundation mortar and vent screens have had decades to wear, which matters more to rodents than the size or price of the house. That is why the walk-through starts at the foundation line and the vents rather than at the part of the house where the noise is loudest.

Regardless of house style, the same process applies here: the technician traces the activity back to its source first, then recommends mouse control or whichever service actually fits, instead of guessing from what was described over the phone.
